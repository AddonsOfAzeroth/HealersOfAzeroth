# Healers of Azeroth

Group frames you heal by clicking, like HealBot and Clique, for WoW Forever, by DinX. Solo, party and raid. Works on its own, and fits the other "of Azeroth" add-ons (Casters of Azeroth's party frames step aside when this one is loaded).

## What it does

- **Clicks**: left, right and middle button, each plain or with Shift, Ctrl or Alt: 12 clicks, each a spell, "target" or "menu". Sensible defaults per class (Priest, Druid, Shaman, Paladin; Mage for decurse and buffs). The first spell you know from a list is used, so it grows with your level.
- **Hover keys** (optional): mouse over a frame and press 1 to 5. With a Razer Naga, pick your side plate (12, 6 or 2 buttons) and the keys Synapse sends: standard (1 to =) or numpad (Num1 to Num+; recommended). Nagas of Azeroth can also learn your buttons and pass them on. The keys get Ctrl and Alt layers (Synapse Hypershift), and Shift with the standard keys (Windows turns Shift + a numpad key into another key, so Shift is off with numpad keys). The spells are kept per Synapse button number, so changing the keys keeps every spell on its button. They only heal while you hover; then they do what they did before (action bars, the Naga bar).
- **Hover everywhere** (on with the hover keys): the same keys also cast on any friendly unit under the mouse: other add-ons' frames, Blizzard's frames, nameplates, characters in the world. Over nothing friendly (or an enemy) a key does what it did before: it clicks its old action bar or Naga button. Only friendly targets, never a harmful spell; one key press is one action. `/hoa hover` shows which keys take part and which are left alone.
- **Frames**: health in class colours, incoming heals, missing health (when the game shows it), a coloured border for a debuff you can dispel, a red edge when a mob is on someone, faded when out of range.
- **Solo, party, raid**: your own frame when alone (to try your clicks), the party, or the whole raid in columns.
- Movable and bigger with the corner grip.

Every heal is your click on Blizzard's own secure buttons: nothing is cast by itself. Clicks change out of combat.

## Commands

- `/hoa`: settings (your clicks)
- `/hoa hover`: which hover keys cast everywhere
- `/hoa toggle`: frames on or off
- `/hoa unlock` / `/hoa lock`: move and resize

# Healers of Azeroth (Nederlands)

Groepsframes die je healt door te klikken, zoals HealBot en Clique: links, rechts en midden, met Shift, Ctrl of Alt. Solo, in een groep en in een raid. Elke heal is jouw klik. Met een Razer Naga volgen de hover-toetsen je zijplaat (12, 6 of 2 knoppen) en de toetsen die Synapse stuurt: standaard (1 tot =) of het numerieke klavier (Num1 tot Num+, aangeraden), ook met Ctrl en Alt. Shift werkt niet met numerieke toetsen (Windows maakt er een andere toets van), dus die laag staat dan uit. De spells blijven per knopnummer bewaard. Met "Overal hoveren" werken die toetsen ook op elke vriendelijke speler of NPC onder de muis (andere frames, naambalkjes, de wereld); op niets vriendelijks doet de toets wat hij vroeger deed.
