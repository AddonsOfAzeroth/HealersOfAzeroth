# Healers of Azeroth

Group frames you heal by clicking, like HealBot and Clique, for WoW Forever, by DinX. Solo, party and raid. Works on its own, and fits the other "of Azeroth" add-ons (Casters of Azeroth's party frames step aside when this one is loaded).

## What it does

- **Clicks**: left, right and middle button, each plain or with Shift, Ctrl or Alt: 12 clicks, each a spell, "target" or "menu". Sensible defaults per class (Priest, Druid, Shaman, Paladin; Mage for decurse and buffs). The first spell you know from a list is used, so it grows with your level.
- **Frames**: health in class colours, incoming heals, missing health (when the game shows it), a coloured border for a debuff you can dispel, a red edge when a mob is on someone, faded when out of range.
- **Solo, party, raid**: your own frame when alone (to try your clicks), the party, or the whole raid in columns.
- Movable and bigger with the corner grip.

Every heal is your click on Blizzard's own secure buttons: nothing is cast by itself. Clicks change out of combat.

## Commands

- `/hoa`: settings (your clicks)
- `/hoa toggle`: frames on or off
- `/hoa unlock` / `/hoa lock`: move and resize
